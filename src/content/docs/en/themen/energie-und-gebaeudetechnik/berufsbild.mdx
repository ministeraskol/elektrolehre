## What does an Elektroniker für Energie- und Gebäudetechnik do?

The **Elektroniker (electronics technician) / Elektronikerin (female electronics technician) for Energie- und Gebäudetechnik (energy and building technology)** (short **EuG**) plans, installs, tests and maintains electrical systems in buildings: from the **Hausanschluss (house connection)** via the **Zählerplatz (meter position)** and the **Unterverteilung (sub-distribution board)** to the last **Steckdose (socket)**. Today, this also includes **Photovoltaik**, **Wallboxen**, **Wärmepumpen**, **Netzwerk- und Gebäudeleittechnik** (Smart Home, KNX) and the regular **Prüfung** of existing systems.

The profession belongs to the **Elektrotechniker-Handwerk** (Annex A No. 25 of the Handwerksordnung). Training takes place in the company and in the **Berufsschule** (dual Ausbildung (apprenticeship)). The **Handwerkskammer (HWK) (Chamber of Skilled Crafts)** is responsible for exams, not the IHK.

There is a second specialization: **Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik**. This page only covers **Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** – the specialization that is by far the most common in the electrical trade.

## The Ausbildung at a glance

| Point | What applies | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal basis** | Ordinance on the vocational training for the Elektroniker and Elektronikerin (**ElekAusbV**) of 30.03.2021 | ElekAusbV 2021 |
| **Duration** | **3½ years** (42 months) | § 2 ElekAusbV |
| **Form** | dual: Company + Berufsschule (block instruction or 1–2 days per week) | BIBB |
| **Specializations** | Energie- und Gebäudetechnik **or** Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik | § 4 ElekAusbV |
| **Exam** | **gestreckte Gesellenprüfung (extended journeyman\'s exam)**: Part 1 in the 4th Ausbildung half-year, Part 2 at the end | § 6 ElekAusbV |
| **Qualification** | Gesellenbrief; designation „Elektroniker/-in Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik" | ElekAusbV 2021 |
| **Responsible body** | Handwerkskammer (HWK) | BIBB |

## What you learn in the Ausbildung

The ElekAusbV divides the Ausbildung into three areas (§ 4):

1. **Cross-specialization skills** – the same for both specializations: analyzing orders, installing electrical systems, commissioning, testing, troubleshooting, instructing customers, documentation.
2. **Skills for the specialization Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** – for example *Designing systems for Energie- und Gebäudetechnik* and *Installing and commissioning energy conversion systems* (Photovoltaik, storage, charging infrastructure).
3. **Integrative skills** – occupational safety, environmental protection, digitalization, rights and duties in the Ausbildung.

What the Berufsschule teaches is listed in the framework curriculum: **13 Lernfelder (learning fields)** with a total of 1,020 teaching hours. You can find the overview on the page [Lernfelder](../lernfelder/).

## The gestreckte Gesellenprüfung

"Gestreckt" means: there is no longer an intermediate exam, but **two exam parts**, both of which count towards the final grade.

| Part | When | Exam area | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** | 4th Ausbildung half-year (after about 1½ years) | **Elektrotechnische Anlagen und Betriebsmittel** – 8 h practical task + 120 min written | **30 %** |
| **Part 2** | End of the Ausbildung | **Kundenauftrag (customer order)** – 16 h practical task with situational technical discussion (max. 20 min) | **36 %** |
| | | **Systementwurf (system design)** (written) | 12 % |
| | | **Funktions- und Systemanalyse (functional and system analysis)** (written) | 12 % |
| | | **Wirtschafts- und Sozialkunde (economics and social studies)** (written) | 10 % |

You have **passed** (§ 15 ElekAusbV) if

- the overall result from Part 1 and Part 2 is at least „ausreichend" (sufficient),
- Part 2 on its own is at least „ausreichend",
- the **Kundenauftrag** is at least „ausreichend",
- at least two other exam areas of Part 2 are „ausreichend" and
- no exam area of Part 2 is „ungenügend" (insufficient).

The Kundenauftrag is thus the centerpiece: it counts the most and must be passed individually. That is exactly why we practice instructions on this page, such as the [Unterverteilung](../unterverteilung/), the way they are required in an exam – with planning, assembly, testing and protocol.

## Requirements

Legally, **no specific school leaving certificate** is prescribed. In practice, most companies hire with a **Hauptschulabschluss** or **mittlerem Schulabschluss (Realschule)**. More important than the degree are:

- **Mathematics and Physics** at a solid level – **Ohmsches Gesetz (Ohm\'s law)**, **Leistung (power)**, rule of three, units. We explain the basics from scratch.
- **German** – orders, standards, test protocols and the exam are in German. If German is not your native language: this page explains in your language, but every Fachbegriff remains German. This way you learn both at the same time.
- **Care and safety awareness** – mistakes in electrical systems are life-threatening.
- **Physical suitability** – working on ladders, in tight spaces, on construction sites.
- **Color vision** – conductor colors (brown, black, grey, blue, green-yellow) must be reliably distinguished.

For applicants from abroad: a foreign school leaving certificate is generally not an obstacle; the company decides. Language level **B1–B2** is realistically necessary because the Berufsschule teaches in German from day one.

## Ausbildung remuneration

There are two lower limits: the statutory **Mindestausbildungsvergütung (minimum apprenticeship remuneration)** (§ 17 BBiG) and – where a collective agreement applies – the **Tarif (collective agreement rate)**. The Tarif in the electrical trade is significantly above the statutory minimum.

| Ausbildung year | Statutory minimum remuneration 2026 (gross/month) | Tarif Elektrohandwerk Baden-Württemberg from 1.3.2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 1st year | 724 € | 1 140 € |
| 2nd year | 854 € | 1 190 € |
| 3rd year | 977 € | 1 290 € |
| 4th year | 1 014 € | 1 390 € |

The minimum remuneration applies to all Ausbildungen that **begin in 2026**, for the entire duration of the Ausbildung. The Tarif in other federal states differs; ask about the applicable collective agreement when applying. A company without a collective agreement may stay at most **20 % below the Tarif**, otherwise the remuneration is considered inappropriate (§ 17 Para. 4 BBiG).

## Finding an Ausbildung position

- **e-zubis.de** – Ausbildung portal of the E-Handwerke (ZVEH) with a job finder by postal code.
- **Job board of the Federal Employment Agency** – search for „Elektroniker Energie- und Gebäudetechnik".
- **Apprenticeship board of the Handwerkskammer** in your region.
- **Directly at the company** – many small electrical companies do not advertise. A phone call or an internship often opens more doors than an online application.

An **internship** of one to two weeks is the best test: you see the daily routine, the company sees you.
