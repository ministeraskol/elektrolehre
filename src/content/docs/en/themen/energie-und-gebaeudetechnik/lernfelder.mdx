## What is a Lernfeld?

Vocational school does not teach in classic subjects like "Electrical Engineering" or "Mathematics", but in **Lernfelder** (learning fields). A Lernfeld is a typical **Kundenauftrag** (customer order) from the workplace, where everything necessary is learned: technical calculations, standards, measuring, documentation, and customer communication. Example Lernfeld 2: *Elektrische Systeme planen und installieren* – here you learn about Leitungen (cables), Schutzorgane (protective devices), circuit diagrams, and testing during a residential installation.

The Lernfelder are defined by the **Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK)** (Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs) in the **Rahmenlehrplan** (framework curriculum), resolution of **18.12.2020**. It applies nationwide; the federal states implement it into their own curricula. The Rahmenlehrplan is coordinated with the ElekAusbV 2021: Lernfelder 1 to 8 are the same for both specializations, while Lernfelder 9 to 13 belong to the specific specialization.

Lernfelder 1 to 8 (Year 1 and 2) are the material for **Teil 1 der Gesellenprüfung** (part 1 of the journeyman's examination) in the 4th half-year of training.

## What is inside the Lernfelder – briefly explained

**Lernfeld 1 – Systeme analysieren, Funktionen prüfen, Fehler beheben.** Einstieg: Stromkreis, Ohmsches Gesetz (Ohm's law), Messen mit dem Multimeter, Schaltpläne lesen, einfache Fehler finden. Die Basis für alles Weitere.

**Lernfeld 2 – Elektrische Systeme planen und installieren.** Residential installation: Leitungen and cross-sections, Ausschaltung (single-pole switching), Wechselschaltung (two-way switching), Steckdose (socket outlet), Leitungsschutzschalter (miniature circuit breaker), DIN 18015, Erstprüfung (initial verification). Our guide [Wechselschaltung und Steckdose](../wechselschaltung-steckdose/) belongs here.

**Lernfeld 3 – Steuerungen und Regelungen.** Contactor, relay, push-button, circuit diagram in expanded representation, first PLC basics (LOGO!, logic functions).

**Lernfeld 4 – Informationstechnische Systeme.** PC, network, IP addresses, data cables (Cat. 7), documentation with software.

**Lernfeld 5 – Elektroenergieversorgung und Sicherheit.** Drehstrom (three-phase current), Netzsysteme (TN, TT, IT), Schutzmaßnahmen nach DIN VDE 0100-410, Schutzklassen, Verteilungsanlagen, Prüfen von Betriebsmitteln. Die Anleitungen [Unterverteilung](../unterverteilung/) (sub-distribution board) und [Zählerplatz](../zaehlerplatz/) (metering point) sind Teil dieses Lernfelds.

**Lernfeld 6 – Systeme analysieren und prüfen.** Measuring and testing according to DIN VDE 0100-600 and DIN VDE 0105-100, test reports, periodic inspections.

**Lernfeld 7 – Steuerungen programmieren.** PLC programming, sensor technology, actuator technology, drives, frequency converters.

**Lernfeld 8 – Energiewandlungssysteme.** Photovoltaics, storage, heat pump, charging infrastructure, grid feed-in – new in the 2020 Rahmenlehrplan and core business in everyday life today.

**Lernfeld 9 EG – Kommunikation in Gebäuden.** Building automation (KNX, wireless), door intercom systems, network and antenna technology in residential and commercial buildings.

**Lernfeld 10 EG – Geräte und Anlagen der Haustechnik.** Stove, instantaneous water heater, ventilation, lighting systems, commissioning and handover to the customer.

**Lernfeld 11 EG – Energietechnische Systeme.** Hausanschluss, Zähleranlagen nach VDE-AR-N 4100, Ersatzstrom, Blitz- und Überspannungsschutz, Instandhaltung.

**Lernfeld 12 EG – Anlagen planen und realisieren.** Larger projects from planning to acceptance; preparation for the customer order in Teil 2 der Prüfung.

**Lernfeld 13 EG – Systeme anpassen und dokumentieren.** Conversion, expansion, energy efficiency, documentation and handover – the finishing touches before the Gesellenprüfung (journeyman's examination).

## How to use this page with the Lernfelder

Every guide on wattwas is assigned to one or more Lernfelder in the page header (`lernfeld`). If you are currently in Lernfeld 5 at vocational school, read the basics on [Netz und Leiterfarben](../../../grundlagen/netz-und-leiterfarben/) and [Schutzorgane](../../../grundlagen/schutzorgane/) and then the guide Unterverteilung.
