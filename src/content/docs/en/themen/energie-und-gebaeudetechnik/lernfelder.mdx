## What is a Lernfeld (learning field)?

The Berufsschule (vocational school) does not teach in classic subjects like "Electrical Engineering" or "Mathematics", but in **Lernfelder**. A Lernfeld is a typical Kundenauftrag (customer order) from the company, where everything necessary is learned: technical calculation, standards, measuring, documentation, and customer consultation. Example Lernfeld 2: *Planning and installing electrical systems* – there you learn about cables, Schutzorgane (protective devices), circuit diagrams, and testing in a residential installation.

The Lernfelder are determined by the Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK) in the Rahmenlehrplan (framework curriculum), decision of 18.12.2020. It applies nationwide; the federal states implement it into their own curricula. The Rahmenlehrplan is coordinated with the ElekAusbV 2021: Lernfelder 1 to 8 are the same for both specializations, while Lernfelder 9 to 13 belong to the specific specialization.

Lernfelder 1 to 8 (Year 1 and 2) are the material for **Part 1 of the Gesellenprüfung** (journeyman’s examination) in the 4th year of Ausbildung.

## What is inside the Lernfelder – explained briefly

**Lernfeld 1 – Analyze systems, check functions, troubleshoot.** Introduction: circuit, Ohmsches Gesetz (Ohm’s law), measuring with a multimeter, reading Stromlaufplan (circuit diagrams), finding simple errors. The basis for everything else.

**Lernfeld 2 – Plan and install electrical systems.** Residential installation: cables and cross-sections, Ausschaltung (single-pole switching), Wechselschaltung (two-way switching), Steckdose (socket), Leitungsschutzschalter (circuit breaker), DIN 18015, Erstprüfung (initial inspection). Our guide [Wechselschaltung und Steckdose](../wechselschaltung-steckdose/) belongs here.

**Lernfeld 3 – Controls and regulation.** Contactor, relay, push button, Stromlaufplan in expanded representation, first PLC basics (LOGO!, logic functions).

**Lernfeld 4 – Information technology systems.** PC, network, IP addresses, data cables (Cat. 7), documentation with software.

**Lernfeld 5 – Electrical energy supply and safety.** Drehstrom (three-phase current), network systems (TN, TT, IT), protective measures according to DIN VDE 0100, protection classes, distribution systems, testing of equipment. The guides [Unterverteilung](../unterverteilung/) and [Zählerplatz](../zaehlerplatz/) are part of this Lernfeld.

**Lernfeld 6 – Analyze and test systems.** Measuring and testing according to DIN VDE 0100-600 and DIN VDE 0105-100, test reports, periodic inspections.

**Lernfeld 7 – Program controls.** PLC programming, sensor technology, actuator technology, drives, frequency converters.

**Lernfeld 8 – Energy conversion systems.** Photovoltaics, storage, heat pump, charging infrastructure, grid feed-in – new in the 2020 Rahmenlehrplan and core business in everyday life today.

**Lernfeld 9 EG – Communication in buildings.** Building automation (KNX, wireless), door intercom systems, network and antenna technology in residential and commercial buildings.

**Lernfeld 10 EG – Devices and systems of building technology.** Stove, instantaneous water heater, ventilation, lighting systems, commissioning and handover to the customer.

**Lernfeld 11 EG – Energy technology systems.** Hausanschluss (house connection), meter installations according to VDE-AR-N 4100, emergency power, lightning and Überspannungsschutz (surge protection), maintenance.

**Lernfeld 12 EG – Plan and implement systems.** Larger projects from planning to acceptance; preparation for the Kundenauftrag in Part 2 of the examination.

**Lernfeld 13 EG – Adapt and document systems.** Renovation, expansion, energy efficiency, documentation and handover – the final touch before the Gesellenprüfung.

## How to use this page with the Lernfelder

Every guide on wattwas is assigned to one or more Lernfelder in the page header (`lernfeld`). If you are currently at Lernfeld 5 in your Berufsschule, read the basics for [Netz und Leiterfarben](../../../grundlagen/netz-und-leiterfarben/) and [Schutzorganen](../../../grundlagen/schutzorgane/) and then the guide for Unterverteilung.
