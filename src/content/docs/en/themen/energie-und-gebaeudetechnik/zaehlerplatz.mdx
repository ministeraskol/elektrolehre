:::caution[Only with registration]
The Zählerplatz is the interface to the Netzbetreiber. Pursuant to **§ 13 NAV**, only a company registered in the Installateurverzeichnis (installer directory) may work here. As an Azubi (trainee), you help set up the Zählerschrank (meter cabinet) under supervision – registration, commissioning, and sealing are handled by the company and the Netzbetreiber. Everything ahead of the Hausanschlusskasten is the property of the Netzbetreiber; work is never done there without them.
:::

## What is the Zählerplatz?

The Zählerplatz is the place where Energie (energy) from the grid is transferred to the customer installation and measured. It is located in the Zählerschrank (utility room, basement, hallway) and consists of several panels and areas precisely specified by the **VDE-AR-N 4100** (Technical Connection Rule Low Voltage, TAR for short). The Netzbetreiber supplement this with their Technical Connection Conditions (TAB), referred to by § 14 NAV.

Everything stated here applies to **new** Zählerplätze. Existing installations may remain until they are significantly modified.

## The path of electricity

<ZaehlerplatzSchema />

<Steps>

1. **Hausanschlusskasten.** This is where the grid cable of the Netzbetreiber ends. The NH fuses (service fuses, e.g., 3×63 A) are located inside the Hausanschlusskasten. The Hausanschlusskasten is sealed; pulling the NH fuses is the responsibility of the Netzbetreiber or only done by arrangement.

2. **Hauptleitung.** From the Hausanschlusskasten to the Zählerschrank, unmetered. Querschnitt (cross-section) according to Leistung (power), usually NYY-J or **NYM-J** **5×16 mm²** or more. It must be installed short-circuit proof and without branches; the Hauptleitung must not run through other people's apartments.

3. **Lower connection compartment.** In the Zählerschrank, the Hauptleitung connects to the main branch terminal or a busbar. From there, each Zählerfeld (meter panel) is supplied. This is also where the connection to the **Haupterdungsschiene** (main earthing busbar) (HES) and the splitting of the PEN into PE and N (TN-C-S) take place.

4. **SLS-Schalter (selective main circuit breaker).** A selective main circuit breaker (e.g., 3×35 A, E characteristic) sits in front of each Zähler (meter). It is the Vorsicherung (backup fuse) of the apartment and the **Freischaltpunkt** (isolation point) for the electrical contractor. Unlike the Hausanschluss (service connection), it can be operated without the Netzbetreiber.

5. **Zählerfeld.** The Zähler is mounted on the Zählerplatz: today mostly as an eHZ (electronic household meter, plug-in technology) or on a three-point mounting (BKE-I adapter). The Zähler belongs to the metering point operator, is installed by them, and sealed. The electrical company provides the finished, tested wiring up to the Zähler terminals.

6. **Upper connection compartment / distribution panel.** Behind the Zähler, the customer installation begins: **Hauptschalter** (main switch), optionally SPD Type 1/2, RCDs and **LS-Schalter** (circuit breaker) – or just the feeder to the **Unterverteilung** (sub-distribution board) of the apartment (see [Unterverteilung aufbauen](../unterverteilung/)).

7. **Space for additional applications (RfZ) and APZ (lockable space for additional applications).** The VDE-AR-N 4100 requires space for a smart meter gateway, control box, and communication technology (**APZ** – lockable space for additional applications, at least 300 mm high, with its own 230 V circuit). Without these spaces, the Netzbetreiber will not accept the Zählerplatz.

</Steps>

## Requirements for the Zählerschrank

| Item | Requirement (VDE-AR-N 4100, TAB) |
|---|---|
| **Cabinet** | Tested Zählerschrank according to DIN VDE 0603 / DIN 43870, **Schutzart** (degree of protection) at least IP31 (basement often IP44), **Schutzklasse** (protection class) II |
| **Height** | Meter center between 0.8 m and 1.8 m above floor |
| **Access** | Freely accessible, 1.2 m clearance in front of the cabinet, not in bathroom, toilet, or damp rooms |
| **Lighting** | Zählerplatz must be readable – **Steckdose** (socket outlet) and lighting in the utility room |
| **Labeling** | Each Zählerfeld labeled with the apartment / connection user, permanently |
| **Isolation** | SLS-Schalter in front of each Zähler, all-pole disconnectable |
| **Überspannungsschutz** (surge protection) | SPD Type 1 or Type 1/2 in the Zählerschrank for new buildings (**DIN VDE 0100**-443/-534), space must be provided |
| **Backup power / PV** | Additional fields for generation systems and storage with separate metering according to VDE-AR-N 4105 |

## Procedure in the company

<Steps>

1. Registration with the Netzbetreiber. Before starting, the company registers the connection or modification (grid connection portal). The Netzbetreiber approves the connected load, meter type, and date.

2. Planning. Number of Zählerfelder (one per apartment, plus general power, heat pump, wallbox), Querschnitt of the Hauptleitung, position of the cabinet, APZ, and RfZ.

3. **Fünf Sicherheitsregeln** (Five safety rules). During remodeling: **Freischalten** (isolating) at the Hausanschlusskasten only with the Netzbetreiber. During new construction: Hauptleitung is not yet connected.

4. Mounting the cabinet and connecting the Hauptleitung. Wires with **Aderendhülsen** (wire end ferrules) or according to manufacturer specifications, observe torque, PEN to the PEN busbar, splitting into PE and N.

5. Equipping the panels. SLS-Schalter, Zählerplatz with terminals, feeder to the Unterverteilung, APZ circuit, labeling.

6. Testing. **Erstprüfung** (initial testing) according to DIN VDE 0100-600: PE continuity, **Isolationswiderstand** (insulation resistance), **Drehfeld** (phase sequence) (clockwise rotating field at the Zähler!), protocol.

7. Completion notification. The company reports the installation with the commissioning protocol and the signature of the responsible **Elektrofachkraft** (qualified electrician). The Netzbetreiber or metering point operator installs the Zähler, seals it, and energizes it.

</Steps>

## Common errors

- **Drehfeld incorrect.** A clockwise rotating field (L1–L2–L3) must be present at the Zähler. The Netzbetreiber checks this during commissioning; incorrect Drehfeld means: no energization.
- **No APZ / RfZ.** The most common reason for rejected acceptance in new buildings.
- **Hauptleitung too small or with branch.** Must withstand the **Nennstrom** (rated current) of the NH fuse.
- **PEN not cleanly split** – PE and N reconnected after splitting.
- **Zähler not readable** (too high, obstructed, behind doors).
- **Seal broken.** Even "just for a moment" is manipulation – prosecuted as power theft.
