:::caution[Nur mit Eintragung]
Der Zählerplatz is the interface to the network operator. According to **§ 13 NAV** only a company listed in the **Installateurverzeichnis** may work here. As an Azubi you build the Zählerschrank under supervision – the registration, commissioning and sealing are handled by the company and the network operator. Everything before the Hausanschlusskasten (HAK) belongs to the network operator; no work is done there without them.
:::

## What is the Zählerplatz?

The **Zählerplatz** is the place where **Energie (energy)** passes from the grid into the customer installation and is **measured**. It is located in the **Zählerschrank** (house connection room, cellar, hallway) and consists of several fields and compartments defined precisely by **VDE-AR-N 4100** (Technical Connection Rule Low Voltage, short TAR). The network operators supplement it with their **Technischen Anschlussbedingungen (TAB)**, referenced by § 14 NAV.

All statements apply to **new** Zählerplätze. Existing installations may stay until they are significantly changed.

## The Path of the Power

<ZaehlerplatzSchema />

<Steps>

1. **Hausanschluss (house connection)kasten (HAK).** Here the network operator’s cable ends. Inside the HAK are the **NH-Sicherungen** (house connection fuses, e.g. 3×63 A). The HAK is **verplombt**; pulling the NH-Sicherungen is the network operator’s job or only done after agreement.

2. **Hauptleitung.** From the HAK to the Zählerschrank, **ungezählt**. Cross‑section according to **Leistung (load)**, usually NYY‑J or NYM‑J **5×16 mm²** or larger. It must be **kurzschlussfest** and installed without any branch; the Hauptleitung may not run through other apartments.

3. **Unterer Anschlussraum.** In the Zählerschrank the Hauptleitung connects to the **Hauptleitungsabzweigklemme** or a **Sammelschiene**. From there each Zählerfeld is supplied. This area also contains the connection to the **Haupterdungsschiene (HES)** and the splitting of the PEN into PE and N (TN‑C‑S).

4. **SLS-Schalter.** In front of every meter there is a **selektiver Hauptleitungsschutzschalter** (e.g. 3×35 A, E‑characteristic). It is the **Vorsicherung (pre‑fuse)** for the apartment and the **Freischaltpunkt** for the electrical operation. Unlike the HAK, it may be operated without the network operator.

5. **Zählerfeld.** The meter is mounted on the **Zählerplatz**: today usually as an **eHZ** (electronic household meter, plug‑in type) or on a **Drei-Punkt-Befestigung** (BKE‑I‑Adapter). The meter belongs to the **Messstellenbetreiber**, is installed by them and **verplombt**. The electrical contractor provides the finished, inspected wiring up to the meter terminals.

6. **Oberer Anschlussraum / Verteilerfeld.** Behind the meter the **Kundenanlage** starts: **Hauptschalter (main switch)**, possibly SPD type 1/2, RCDs and LS‑Schalter – or only the outgoing line to the **Unterverteilung** of the apartment (see [Unterverteilung aufbauen](../unterverteilung/)).

7. **Raum für Zusatzanwendungen (RfZ) and APZ.** VDE-AR-N 4100 requires space for a **Smart‑Meter‑Gateway**, control box and communication technology (**APZ** – lockable space for additional applications, at least 300 mm high, with its own 230‑V circuit). Without these rooms the network operator will not accept the Zählerplatz.

</Steps>

## Requirements for the Zählerschrank

| Point | Requirement (VDE-AR-N 4100, TAB) |
|---|---|
| **Schrank** | certified Zählerschrank according to DIN VDE 0603 / DIN 43870, protection class at least IP31 (cellars often IP44), protection class II |
| **Height** | meter centre between 0.8 m and 1.8 m above floor |
| **Access** | freely accessible, 1.2 m clearance in front of the cabinet, not in bathroom, toilet or damp rooms |
| **Lighting** | Zählerplatz must be readable – **Steckdose (socket)** and lighting in the house connection room |
| **Labeling** | each Zählerfeld labelled with the apartment / user, permanent |
| **Disconnection** | SLS in front of each meter, all‑pole disconnectable |
| **Surge protection** | **Überspannungsschutz (SPD)** type 1 or type 1/2 in the Zählerschrank for new builds (DIN VDE 0100‑443/-534), space must be provided |
| **Backup power / PV** | additional fields for generation plants and storage with own measurement according to VDE-AR-N 4105 |

## Process in the Company

<Steps>

1. **Registration with the network operator.** Before starting the company registers the connection or change (network connection portal). The network operator approves the connection power, meter type and date.

2. **Planning.** Number of meters (one per apartment, plus common power, heat pump, wallbox), cross‑section of the Hauptleitung, cabinet position, APZ and RfZ.

3. **Five safety rules.** During renovation: disconnect at the HAK only with the network operator. For new construction: the Hauptleitung is not yet connected.

4. **Mount cabinet and connect Hauptleitung.** Strands with **Aderendhülsen (end sleeves)** or as per manufacturer, observe torque, PEN to the PEN‑bus, split into PE and N.

5. **Populate fields.** SLS, Zählerplatz with terminals, outgoing line to Unterverteilung, APZ circuit, labeling.

6. **Testing.** **Erstprüfung (first inspection)** according to DIN VDE 0100‑600: continuity of PE, insulation resistance, rotation field (right‑hand rotation at the meter!), protocol.

7. **Completion report.** The company reports the installation with the **Inbetriebsetzungsprotokoll** and the signature of the responsible **Elektrofachkraft**. The network operator or Messstellenbetreiber installs the meter, seals it and switches it on.

</Steps>

## Common Mistakes

- **Wrong rotation field.** The meter must have a **Rechtsdrehfeld** (L1–L2–L3). The network operator checks this during commissioning; wrong rotation means no connection.
- **No APZ / RfZ.** The most common reason for a rejected acceptance in new builds.
- **Hauptleitung too small or with a branch.** Must withstand the rated current of the NH‑Sicherung.
- **PEN not cleanly split** – PE and N re‑connected after the split.
- **Meter not readable** (too high, obstructed, behind doors).
- **Seal broken.** Even a “quick” break is tampering – treated as electricity theft.
