## The three basic circuits

Almost every residential installation consists of three building blocks that you will wire in your first year of apprenticeship until you can do them in your sleep:

| Circuit | Task | Switch | Cores to the lamp |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ausschaltung** | switch a lamp from **one** location | 1 switch (2 terminals: L, Geschalteter Leiter) | L (switched), N, PE |
| **Wechselschaltung** | switch a lamp from **two** locations (hallway, bedroom) | 2 switches (each 3 terminals: L, two Korrespondierende Leiter) | as above; between the switches 2 Korrespondierende Leiter |
| **Steckdose** | connection for mobile devices | none | L, N, PE |

For **three or more** switching points, the **Kreuzschaltung** is added (one intermediate switch between two switches) – own guide to follow.

## Ausschaltung

The simplest circuit. The Außenleiter (phase/line conductor) **L** (brown) goes to the switch, from the switch the **Geschalteter Leiter** (switched conductor) (black) goes to the lamp, from the lamp the **N** (neutral conductor) (blue) goes directly back. **PE** (protective earth) (green-yellow) goes to the lamp if it has a Schutzleiter connection – and always to the socket.

Rule number one of installation: **Always switch the Außenleiter, never the N.** If the N is switched, the lamp will still light up, but it remains under Spannung when "off" – life-threatening when changing a bulb.

Cables: **NYM-J 3×1,5 mm²** from the Verteilerdose to the switch (brown = L in, black = switched back, carry PE along, blue remains unused and insulated or is used as N for a Steckdose next to the switch) and **3×1,5 mm²** to the lamp.

## Wechselschaltung

<WechselschaltungSchema />

Two switches each have three terminals: **L** (common contact, often marked with an arrow) and **two outputs**. The switch connects L alternatively to one or the other output.

- **Switch 1:** L (brown) to terminal **L**. The two outputs go as **Korrespondierende Leiter** (black and grey) to Switch 2.
- **Switch 2:** Korrespondierende Leiter to the two outputs. From terminal **L**, the **Geschalteter Leiter** (black) goes to the lamp.
- **Lamp:** Geschalteter Leiter, N, PE.

No matter the position: One switch always changes the state. If both switches are on the same Korrespondierende Leiter, the circuit is closed – light on.

Cables: **NYM-J 5×1,5 mm²** between the switches (brown L, black and grey corresponding, blue N for a Steckdose, PE) or 3×1,5 mm² if there is no Steckdose at the switch.

**Check the L terminal at the switch with a continuity tester** before you connect – manufacturers mark them differently. If you swap L and a corresponding connection, the circuit will only work in one position.

## Steckdose (protective contact)

The **Schuko-Steckdose** has three terminals: **L**, **N** and **PE** (protective contact, the two clips). In Germany, the Steckdose is not polarity-protected – L can be on the left or right. Common (but not a standard) is **N left, L right** when looking at the Steckdose.

Rules:

- **Always connect PE** – even if the device only has two poles. A Steckdose without PE is a defect.
- **Every Steckdose in the apartment behind an RCD 30 mA** (DIN VDE 0100-410).
- **B16** with **2,5 mm²**. 1,5 mm² with B16 only for short cables (up to about 15 m when installed flush-mounted) – Berufsschule will calculate this.
- **Steckklemmen** (plug-in terminals) in modern Steckdosen: strip wire **10–12 mm**, insert straight, pull test. For screw terminals: torque according to manufacturer specifications.
- Steckdosen in the **bathroom**: observe protection zones according to DIN VDE 0100-701 – distance of 60 cm to the bathtub / shower.
- **Minimum equipment** according to DIN 18015-2: for example, a living room up to 20 m² requires at least 5 Steckdosen, a kitchen at least 7 (of which 3 are above the worktop). If you plan too few, everything will end up on multiple Steckdosen later.

## Wiring step by step (practice wall or apartment)

<Steps>

1. **Fünf Sicherheitsregeln**. Turn off LS (circuit breaker), use a lockout device, check for voltage-free state at the Verteilerdose and all boxes bipolarly.

2. **Read the plan.** Stromlaufplan (circuit diagram) in **exploded view** and **Installationsplan** (where everything is located). Check off wires and terminals on the plan before you connect.

3. **Pull in the cables.** From the Verteilerdose in a star pattern: to Switch 1, Switch 2, lamp, Steckdose. Cut the cable with 15–20 cm reserve in each box. Cable routing in **Installationszonen** (horizontally 30 cm below the ceiling or above the floor, vertically 15 cm next to doors and corners).

4. **Stripping and insulation removal.** Remove the sheath without nicking the wires, strip wires **10–12 mm** (observe terminal specifications). Do not damage the copper strands.

5. **Connect the Verteilerdose.** With **Verbindungsklemmen** (connection terminals): all N together, all PE together, L to the outgoing L-conductor to Switch 1, connect Korrespondierende Leiter through, Geschalteter Leiter to the lamp. **No conductor may lie loose in the box.**

6. **Connect switches and Steckdose.** Terminals according to printing. Confirm L at the Wechselschalter with a continuity tester. PE at the Steckdose first.

7. **Connect the lamp.** Geschalteter Leiter to L of the lamp, N to N, PE to PE. Even with Schutzklasse-II lamps, keep PE ready in the box (insulate it).

8. **Test before switching on.** See next section.

9. **Switch on and check function.** Test Wechselschaltung from all four combinations. Check the Steckdose with a socket tester.

10. **Labeling.** Enter the circuit in the distribution board.

</Steps>

## Testing according to DIN VDE 0100-600

Even the smallest circuit is tested and documented:

- **Inspection:** Colors correct, PE everywhere, no damaged wires, boxes closed, covers on.
- **Continuity of the Schutzleiter:** from the PE terminal in the distribution board to the Schutzkontakt of the Steckdose and to the PE of the lamp, low resistance (typically below 1 Ω).
- **Isolationswiderstand**: all conductors against PE, 500 V DC, at least **1 MΩ**. Disconnect or unscrew lamps and devices beforehand.
- **Schleifenimpedanz** at the Steckdose: the LS must trip in the case of a Kurzschluss (B16: Kurzschlussstrom at least 80 A).
- **RCD test**: tripping time and tripping current at the Steckdose.
- **Drehfeld** is not applicable for 230 V.

## Common errors

- **N switched instead of L.** It works, but it is life-threatening.
- **L and corresponding connection at the Wechselschalter swapped.** The circuit only works "sometimes".
- **PE not connected** or used as L in the box.
- **Blue wire used as Geschalteter Leiter** without marking.
- **Wire stripped too little** – terminal grips the insulation: loose contact, heat.
- **Wire stripped too much** – bare copper outside the terminal: danger of contact, Kurzschluss.
