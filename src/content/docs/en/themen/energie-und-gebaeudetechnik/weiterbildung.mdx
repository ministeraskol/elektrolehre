## After the Gesellenbrief

With your passed Gesellenprüfung (journeyman's examination), you are an Elektroniker/-in Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik (electronics technician specializing in energy and building technology) and at Niveau 4 (level 4) in the Deutschen Qualifikationsrahmen (DQR) (German Qualifications Framework). From here, three major paths lead further: Meister (master craftsman), Techniker (technician), or Studium (university studies). All three are possible without Abitur (high school diploma) – that is the strength of the German system.

| Path | Degree | DQR | Typical Duration | What it brings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meisterprüfung** | Elektrotechnikmeister/-in, additionally „Bachelor Professional in Elektrotechnik" | 6 | 1 year full-time or approx. 2 years part-time | Found and manage a business, train Azubis (apprentices), entry in the Installateurverzeichnis (installer directory) |
| **Fachschule** | Staatlich geprüfte/-r Techniker/-in Elektrotechnik | 6 | 2 years full-time or 4 years part-time | Planning, project management, construction management, industry |
| **Studium** | Bachelor of Engineering (Elektrotechnik, Gebäudetechnik) | 6 | 3–3½ years | Development, planning office, engineering career |

Meister, Techniker, and Bachelor are all at DQR-Niveau 6. This means they are gleichwertig (equivalent), not gleichartig (similar): the Meister is the practitioner and entrepreneur, the Techniker the planner, and the engineer the developer.

## The Meister

The Meisterprüfung is regulated by § 45 of the Handwerksordnung (HwO) (Crafts Code). It has **four parts**:

1. **Teil I – Fachpraxis (practical expertise):** a Meisterprüfungsprojekt (master craftsman examination project) (for example, planning and executing a complete building installation) with a Fachgespräch (technical discussion) and a situational task.
2. **Teil II – Fachtheorie (technical theory):** Elektrotechnik, Anlagen- und Systemtechnik, Auftragsabwicklung, Betriebsführung.
3. **Teil III – Betriebswirtschaft, Kaufmännisches und Recht.**
4. **Teil IV – Berufs- und Arbeitspädagogik** (vocational and work pedagogy) (trainer suitability, AEVO).

Those who have passed the Gesellenprüfung in the Elektrotechniker-Handwerk (electrical engineering trade) are admitted; there has no longer been a waiting period since 2004. Anyone who passes the examination may additionally call themselves „Bachelor Professional in Elektrotechnik" (§ 51 Abs. 2 HwO) – not an academic degree, but the official classification at Bachelor level.

**Why the Meister is particularly important in the electrical trade:** The Elektrotechniker-Handwerk is zulassungspflichtig (subject to licensing) (Anlage A HwO). Anyone who wants to run an electrical business independently needs the Meister or an equivalent qualification. And only a registered business is allowed to work on the Hausanschluss (house connection) and the Zähler (meter) (§ 13 NAV, Installateurverzeichnis of the network operator).

Preparation costs money (courses, examination fees, materials). The Aufstiegs-BAföG (AFBG) (advancement training grant) promotes Meister and Techniker courses with grants and loans; after passing the examination, part of the loan is forgiven. Information is provided by the responsible authority in your federal state.

## The Staatlich geprüfte Techniker

The Fachschule für Technik (technical college) (specializing in Elektrotechnik, often with a focus on energy and building technology or automation) teaches in **two years full-time** (or four years part-time) what is often missed in the Ausbildung (apprenticeship): planning, calculation, project management, and standards in depth. The prerequisite is the Gesellenbrief plus usually **one year of professional experience**.

With the Techniker degree, you also receive the Fachhochschulreife (entrance qualification for universities of applied sciences) in most federal states. Techniker work in planning offices, in construction management, in industry, for network operators – and usually earn significantly more than Gesellen (journeymen).

## Studium without Abitur

Since the KMK resolution of **March 6, 2009**, the following applies nationwide:

- **Meister and Techniker** have the allgemeine Hochschulzugangsberechtigung (general university entrance qualification) – they are allowed to study any subject at any university.
- **Gesellen with at least three years of professional experience** receive a fachgebundene Hochschulzugangsberechtigung (subject-linked university entrance qualification) for a subject that fits their profession (for example, Elektrotechnik, Gebäudetechnik) – usually after an aptitude interview or an examination.

Popular degree programs after the Ausbildung: **Elektrotechnik**, **Energie- und Gebäudetechnik**, **Gebäudeautomation**, **Regenerative Energien**. Many universities offer them part-time or dual.

## Specializations without a new degree

Not everyone wants to become a Meister. In the electrical trade, there are in-demand specializations that run through manufacturer courses or certificates:

- **Photovoltaik und Speicher (photovoltaics and storage)** – planning, installation, grid connection, registration with the network operator.
- **Ladeinfrastruktur (charging infrastructure) (Wallbox, Ladeparks)** – load management, billing, grid registration.
- **Gebäudeautomation (building automation) (KNX-Partner, Smart Home)** – certification by the KNX Association.
- **Prüfen ortsveränderlicher und ortsfester Anlagen (testing portable and stationary systems)** – DGUV Vorschrift 3, DIN VDE 0701/0702, as an Elektrofachkraft (qualified electrician).
- **Wärmepumpe und Sektorkopplung (heat pump and sector coupling)** – cooperation with SHK-Betrieben (HVAC companies).
- **Brandmelde- und Sicherheitstechnik (fire alarm and security technology)** – specialist according to DIN 14675.

Each of these directions is so much in demand today that companies usually pay for the training.

## How you decide

- **You want your own contracts, your own business, responsibility for people?** → Meister.
- **You want to plan and calculate, less construction site, more office?** → Techniker.
- **You want to develop, research, go into industry or an engineering office?** → Studium.
- **You want to earn money first and get good?** → two to three years as Geselle, then a specialization. Many Meister started exactly that way.

No path is final. Techniker later do the Meister, Meister study part-time. The Gesellenbrief is the key – the rest is time and will.
