The **Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** (specialisation Energy and Building Technology) is the largest electrical trade in the skilled crafts: a 3½-year dual Ausbildung (apprenticeship) under the ElekAusbV 2021, a Gesellenprüfung (journeyman's examination) in two parts, and afterwards you are an Elektrofachkraft (qualified electrical worker). You build the system that lies behind the wall in every house – from the house connection to the Steckdose (socket outlet).
