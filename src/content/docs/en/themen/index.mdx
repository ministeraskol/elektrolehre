Electrical engineering is not one job, but a family. They all share the same [Grundlagen (fundamentals)](../grundlagen/) and the same
[five safety rules](../grundlagen/sicherheitsregeln/) – everything else is what differs: construction site or Schaltschrank (control cabinet),
apartment or factory, cable or code.

<Themen />

## How you decide

- **You want to see what you've built** (Verteiler (distribution board), lighting, Steckdose (socket outlet), Wallbox)? → Energie- und Gebäudetechnik.
- **You like logic, sensors, SPS (PLC)** and want machines to run on their own? → Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik.
- **Large installations, motors, Schaltschränke in industry**? → Betriebstechnik.
- **Cars, Hochvolt (high voltage), diagnostics**? → Kfz-Mechatronik with a focus on System- und Hochvolttechnik.
- **Smart Home, KNX, the building as a system**? → Gebäudesystemintegration.

No matter where you end up: The Berufsschule (vocational school) teaches the first Lernfelder (learning fields) similarly for all electrical trades. What you learn here in the
[Grundlagen](../grundlagen/) applies everywhere.
