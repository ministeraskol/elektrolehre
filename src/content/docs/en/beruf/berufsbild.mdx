## What does an Elektroniker for Energie- und Gebäudetechnik do?

The **Elektroniker / Elektronikerin der Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** (short: **EuG**) plans, installs, tests and maintains the electrical systems in buildings: from the **Hausanschluss** (house connection) via the **Zählerplatz** (meter position) and the **Unterverteilung** (sub-distribution board) to the last **Steckdose** (socket outlet). On top of that come today's **Photovoltaik** (photovoltaics), **Wallboxen** (wallboxes), **Wärmepumpen** (heat pumps), **Netzwerk- und Gebäudeleittechnik** (network and building automation — smart home, KNX) and the regular **Prüfung** (testing) of existing installations.

The trade belongs to the **Elektrotechniker-Handwerk** (electrical trades; Annex A No. 25 of the Handwerksordnung). Training takes place in the company and in the **Berufsschule** (part-time vocational school) — the dual Ausbildung. The **Handwerkskammer (HWK)** (Chamber of Crafts) is responsible for the exams, not the IHK.

There is a second Fachrichtung: **Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik**. This page covers only **Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** — the Fachrichtung that is by far the most commonly trained one in the Elektrohandwerk.

## The Ausbildung at a glance

| Point | What applies | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal basis** | Verordnung über die Berufsausbildung zum Elektroniker und zur Elektronikerin (**ElekAusbV**) of 30.03.2021 | ElekAusbV 2021 |
| **Duration** | **3½ years** (42 months) | § 2 ElekAusbV |
| **Form** | dual: company + Berufsschule (block lessons or 1–2 days per week) | BIBB |
| **Fachrichtungen** | Energie- und Gebäudetechnik **or** Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik | § 4 ElekAusbV |

| **Qualification** | Gesellenbrief; title „Elektroniker/-in Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik" | ElekAusbV 2021 |
| **Responsible body** | Handwerkskammer (HWK) | BIBB |

## What you learn during the Ausbildung

The ElekAusbV divides the Ausbildung into three areas (§ 4):

1. **Fachrichtungsübergreifende Fertigkeiten** (skills common to all Fachrichtungen) – the same for both Fachrichtungen: analysing jobs, installing, commissioning and testing electrical systems, fault-finding, instructing customers, documentation.
2. **Fertigkeiten der Fachrichtung Energie- und Gebäudetechnik** (skills of the Energie- und Gebäudetechnik Fachrichtung) – for example *Konzipieren von Systemen der Energie- und Gebäudetechnik* (designing energy and building technology systems) and *Installieren und Inbetriebnehmen von Energiewandlungssystemen* (installing and commissioning energy conversion systems — photovoltaics, storage, charging infrastructure).
3. **Integrativ zu vermittelnde Fertigkeiten** (skills taught across the curriculum) – occupational safety, environmental protection, digitalisation, rights and duties during the Ausbildung.

What the Berufsschule teaches alongside this is set out in the Rahmenlehrplan (curriculum framework): **13 Lernfelder** (learning fields) with a total of 1 020 Unterrichtsstunden (lessons). You'll find the overview on the [Lernfelder](../lernfelder/) page.

## The gestreckte Gesellenprüfung

"Gestreckt" (stretched) means: there is no longer a separate Zwischenprüfung (intermediate exam); instead there are **two exam parts**, and both count towards the final grade.

| Part | When | Exam area | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Teil 1** | 4. Ausbildungshalbjahr (i.e. after about 1½ years) | **Elektrotechnische Anlagen und Betriebsmittel** (electrical installations and equipment) – 8 h practical task + 120 min written | **30 %** |
| **Teil 2** | End of the Ausbildung | **Kundenauftrag** (customer job) – 16 h practical task with a situational Fachgespräch (technical discussion, max. 20 min) | **36 %** |
| | | **Systementwurf** (system design, written) | 12 % |
| | | **Funktions- und Systemanalyse** (function and system analysis, written) | 12 % |
| | | **Wirtschafts- und Sozialkunde** (business and social studies, written) | 10 % |

You have **passed** (§ 15 ElekAusbV) if

- the overall result from Teil 1 and Teil 2 is at least „ausreichend" (sufficient),
- Teil 2 on its own is at least „ausreichend",
- the **Kundenauftrag** is at least „ausreichend",
- at least two further exam areas of Teil 2 are „ausreichend", and
- no exam area of Teil 2 is „ungenügend" (insufficient).

That makes the Kundenauftrag the heart of the exam: it carries the most weight and must be passed on its own. That's exactly why on this site we practise guides like the [Unterverteilung](../../anleitungen/unterverteilung/) the way they are demanded in an exam — with planning, build-up, testing and a protocol.

## Requirements

Legally, **no specific school leaving certificate** is required. In practice, most companies hire people with a **Hauptschulabschluss** (lower secondary school certificate) or **mittlerer Schulabschluss (Realschule)** (intermediate school certificate). More important than the certificate:

- **Mathematics and physics** at a solid level – Ohm's law, power, rule of three, units. We explain the basics from scratch.
- **German** – jobs, standards, test protocols and the exam are all in German. If German isn't your first language: this site explains things in your language, but every Fachbegriff (technical term) stays German. That way you learn both at once.
- **Care and safety awareness** – mistakes on electrical installations can be deadly.
- **Physical fitness** – working on ladders, in tight spaces, on construction sites.
- **Colour vision** – conductor colours (brown, black, grey, blue, green-yellow) must be reliably distinguishable.

For applicants from abroad: a foreign school certificate is usually not an obstacle — the company decides. A language level of **B1–B2** is realistically needed, because the Berufsschule teaches in German from day one.

## Ausbildungsvergütung (apprentice pay)

There are two minimums: the statutory **Mindestausbildungsvergütung** (minimum apprentice pay, § 17 BBiG) and — where a Tarifvertrag (collective agreement) applies — the **Tarif**. In the Elektrohandwerk, the Tarif is well above the statutory minimum.

| Ausbildungsjahr | Statutory minimum pay 2026 (gross/month) | Tarif Elektrohandwerk Baden-Württemberg from 1.3.2026 |
|---|---|---|
| 1st year | 724 € | 1 140 € |
| 2nd year | 854 € | 1 190 € |
| 3rd year | 977 € | 1 290 € |
| 4th year | 1 014 € | 1 390 € |

The minimum pay applies to all Ausbildungen that **start in 2026**, for the entire duration of the Ausbildung. The Tarif in other Bundesländer differs; ask about the applicable Tarifvertrag when you apply. A company not bound by a Tarifvertrag may pay at most **20 % below the Tarif**, otherwise the pay counts as not appropriate (§ 17 Abs. 4 BBiG).

## Finding an Ausbildungsplatz (training position)

- **e-zubis.de** – Ausbildungsportal of the E-Handwerke (ZVEH) with a vacancy finder by postal code.
- **Jobbörse der Bundesagentur für Arbeit** (job board of the Federal Employment Agency) – search for „Elektroniker Energie- und Gebäudetechnik".
- **Lehrstellenbörse der Handwerkskammer** (apprenticeship board of the Chamber of Crafts) in your region.
- **Directly at the company** – many small electrical companies don't advertise openings. A phone call or an internship often opens more doors than an online application.

A **Praktikum** (internship) of one to two weeks is the best test: you see the everyday work, and the company sees you.
