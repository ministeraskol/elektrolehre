## What is a Lernfeld?

The Berufsschule (part-time vocational school) does not teach classic subjects like "electrical engineering" or "mathematics", but rather **Lernfelder** (learning fields). A Lernfeld is a typical **Kundenauftrag** (customer job) from the company, through which everything necessary is learned: technical calculations, standards, measuring, documentation, talking to customers. Example Lernfeld 2: *Elektrische Systeme planen und installieren* (plan and install electrical systems) – there you learn about wiring, protective devices, circuit diagrams and testing, using a residential installation as the example.

The Lernfelder are defined by the **Kultusministerkonferenz (KMK)** (Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education) in the **Rahmenlehrplan** (framework curriculum), resolution of **18.12.2020**. It applies nationwide; the federal states implement it in their own curricula. The Rahmenlehrplan is coordinated with the ElekAusbV 2021: Lernfelder 1 to 8 are the same for both specialisations, Lernfelder 9 to 13 belong to the specialisation.

Lernfelder 1 to 8 (years 1 and 2) are the material for **Teil 1 der Gesellenprüfung** (part 1 of the journeyman examination) in the 4th half-year of the Ausbildung (apprenticeship).

## What is in the Lernfelder – briefly explained

**Lernfeld 1 – Analyse systems, test functions, rectify faults.** Getting started: the circuit, Ohm's law, measuring with the multimeter, reading circuit diagrams, finding simple faults. The foundation for everything else.

**Lernfeld 2 – Plan and install electrical systems.** The residential installation: cables and cross-sections, switching off, Wechselschaltung (two-way switching circuit), Steckdose (socket outlet), Leitungsschutzschalter (miniature circuit breaker), DIN 18015, Erstprüfung (initial inspection). Our guide [Wechselschaltung und Steckdose](../../anleitungen/wechselschaltung-steckdose/) belongs here.

**Lernfeld 3 – Controls and regulations.** Contactor, relay, push-button, the ladder-style schematic diagram, first PLC basics (LOGO!, logic functions).

**Lernfeld 4 – Information technology systems.** PC, network, IP addresses, data cables (Cat. 7), documentation with software.

**Lernfeld 5 – Power supply and safety.** Three-phase current, network systems (TN, TT, IT), protective measures according to DIN VDE 0100-410, protection classes, distribution installations, testing of operating equipment. The guides [Unterverteilung](../../anleitungen/unterverteilung/) (sub-distribution board) and [Zählerplatz](../../anleitungen/zaehlerplatz/) (meter position) are material from this Lernfeld.

**Lernfeld 6 – Analyse and test systems.** Measuring and testing according to DIN VDE 0100-600 and DIN VDE 0105-100, test protocols, repeat tests.

**Lernfeld 7 – Programming controls.** PLC programming, sensorics, actuatorics, drives, frequency converters.

**Lernfeld 8 – Energy conversion systems.** Photovoltaics, battery storage, heat pump, charging infrastructure, grid feed-in – new in the Rahmenlehrplan 2020 and today core business in everyday work.

**Lernfeld 9 EG – Communication in buildings.** Building automation (KNX, radio), door intercom systems, network and antenna technology in residential and functional buildings.

**Lernfeld 10 EG – Appliances and systems of building services.** Cooker, instantaneous water heater, ventilation, lighting installations, commissioning and handover to the customer.

**Lernfeld 11 EG – Energy technology systems.** House connection, meter installations according to VDE-AR-N 4100, backup power, lightning and surge protection, maintenance.

**Lernfeld 12 EG – Plan and implement installations.** Larger projects from planning to acceptance; preparation for the customer job in part 2 of the examination.

**Lernfeld 13 EG – Adapt and document systems.** Conversion, extension, energy efficiency, documentation and handover – the final polish before the Gesellenprüfung.

## How to use this page with the Lernfelder

Every guide on Elektrolehre is assigned to one or more Lernfelder in the page header (`lernfeld`). If you are currently on Lernfeld 5 at the Berufsschule, read the basics on [Netz und Leiterfarben](../../grundlagen/netz-und-leiterfarben/) (mains systems and conductor colours) and [Schutzorganen](../../grundlagen/schutzorgane/) (protective devices) and afterwards the Unterverteilung guide.
