**First things first, because many people don't know this:** Tools that you need for your Ausbildung (apprenticeship) must be provided to you by the company free of charge – that's what § 14 BBiG says. Persönliche Schutzausrüstung (personal protective equipment) too. So what you'll find here is not a shopping list for your first day, but an orientation guide: what is a must-have in every bag, what makes you faster, and what is pure luxury.
