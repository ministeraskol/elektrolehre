## First the legal situation, then the list

**Your company must provide your Ausbildungsmittel (training materials) free of charge** – tools, materials, technical literature, everything you need for your Ausbildung (apprenticeship) and your exams (§ 14 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 BBiG). **Persönliche Schutzausrüstung (personal protective equipment)** – safety shoes, helmet, safety glasses, gloves – is included too; your employer is not allowed to pass those costs on to you (§ 3 Abs. 3 ArbSchG).

If your company says "go buy yourself a Spannungsprüfer (voltage tester)" – that's their job, not yours. Ask politely.

## The four items that always belong in your bag

| Tool | Why | How to spot quality |
|---|---|---|
| **Zweipoliger Spannungsprüfer** (two-pole voltage tester) | Safety rule 3: proving absence of voltage – only with this, never with a Multimeter (multimeter) alone | DIN EN 61243-3, CAT III or IV, display works without a battery |
| **VDE-Schraubendreher** (VDE-insulated screwdriver) (slotted, PH, PZ) | Every terminal, every switch, every Steckdose (socket outlet) | IEC 60900, insulation right up to the tip, "1000 V" marking |
| **Seitenschneider VDE** (VDE side cutters) | Trimming conductors, cable ties | IEC 60900, the cutting edge fits inside the Verteiler (distribution board) |
| **Abisolierzange** (wire stripper) | Exposing a core without nicking the copper | Self-adjusting, length stop |

Everything else – Crimpzange (crimping pliers), Multimeter, Abmantler (cable sheath stripper), Stirnlampe (headlamp) – comes with the tasks. The overview with pros and cons is in the [tool guide](./).

## Where spending your own money pays off (and where it doesn't)

- **Worth it:** your own set of VDE-Schraubendreher in *your* favourite sizes and a Stirnlampe. You use both every day, both are cheap, and both you get to take home after your Ausbildung.
- **Worth it later:** a Multimeter, once you get into fault finding (Lernfeld (learning field) 5 and up). Before that you'll barely need it.
- **Not worth it:** Akkuschrauber (cordless drill), Bohrhammer (rotary hammer), Kabelrollen (cable drums). That's company equipment, and if it breaks, it's better if it belongs to the company.

## Care = safety

VDE tooling with damaged insulation is no longer a tool – it's a hazard. Once a week: check the insulation for cracks, the cutting edges for nicks, and test your Spannungsprüfer on a known voltage – **before** and **after** every check for absence of voltage (DIN VDE 0105-100).
