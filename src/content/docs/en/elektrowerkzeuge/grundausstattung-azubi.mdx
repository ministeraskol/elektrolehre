## First the legal situation, then the list

**The company must provide your Ausbildung (apprenticeship) materials free of charge** – tools, materials, technical literature, everything you need for your Ausbildung and exams (§ 14 Abs. 1 Nr. 3 BBiG). **Persönliche Schutzausrüstung** (personal protective equipment) (safety shoes, helmet, safety goggles, gloves) as well; the employer may not pass the costs for these on to you (§ 3 Abs. 3 ArbSchG).

If your company says, "Go buy yourself a Spannungsprüfer (voltage tester)" – that is their job, not yours. Ask politely.

## The four items that always belong in your bag

| Tool | Why | How to recognize quality |
|---|---|---|
| **Zweipoliger Spannungsprüfer** | Safety rule 3: Determine absence of Spannung – only with this, never with a multimeter alone | DIN EN 61243-3, CAT III or IV, display without battery |
| **VDE-Schraubendreher** (slotted, PH, PZ) | Every terminal, every switch, every Steckdose (socket) | IEC 60900, insulation up to the tip, "1000 V" imprint |
| **Seitenschneider VDE** | Shorten conductors, cable ties | IEC 60900, cutting edge fits into the distribution board |
| **Abisolierzange** | Expose wire without a notch in the copper | Self-adjusting, length stop |

Everything else – crimping tool, multimeter, cable stripper, headlamp – comes with the tasks. The overview with pros and cons is in the [tool guide](./).

## Where your own money is worth it (and where it isn't)

- **Worth it:** your own set of VDE-Schraubendreher in *your* favorite sizes and a headlamp. You use both daily, both are inexpensive, and you will take both with you after your Ausbildung.
- **Worth it later:** a multimeter, as soon as you are involved in troubleshooting (Lernfeld (learning field) 5 onwards). Before that, you hardly need one.
- **Worth it not:** cordless drill, rotary hammer, cable reels. This is company equipment, and if it breaks, it is better if it belongs to the company.

## Maintenance = Safety

VDE-Werkzeug with damaged insulation is no longer a tool, but a risk. Once a week: check insulation for cracks, cutting edges for nicks, test the Spannungsprüfer on a known Spannung – **before** and **after** every check for absence of Spannung (DIN VDE 0105-100).
