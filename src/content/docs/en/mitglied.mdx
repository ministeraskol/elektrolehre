Being a Mitglied (member) at wattwas means: You get emails that help you move forward in your Ausbildung (apprenticeship) – and you get a say in what gets explained next.

<Mitglied />

## What you get

- **New guides and videos first.** Whatever appears on the site is already in your inbox.
- **Exam sprint.** Before your Zwischenprüfung (intermediate exam) and Gesellenprüfung (journeyman examination): five days, five emails – the mistakes that cost the most points in exams, and the rules you have to know by heart.
- **Your questions.** You can reply to every email. The most common questions become guides, videos and quizzes.
- **Right for your level.** When you sign up, you choose Einstieg (beginner), Geselle (journeyman) or Meister (master) – the content adapts to you.

## What you don't get

No third-party advertising, no daily emails, no passing your address on to anyone. You can unsubscribe with one click in every email; after that, your address is deleted. Details in the [privacy policy](./rechtliches/datenschutz/).
