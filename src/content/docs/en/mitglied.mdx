Being a member at wattwas means: You receive emails that help you progress in your Ausbildung (vocational training) – and you help decide what gets explained next.

<Mitglied />

## What you get

- **New guides and videos first.** You get what appears on the site in your inbox already.
- **Exam sprint.** Before the intermediate and Gesellenprüfung (journeyman\'s examination): five days, five emails – the mistakes that most frequently cost points in exams, and the rules you have to know by heart.
- **Your questions.** You can reply to every email. The most frequent questions become guides, videos, and quizzes.
- **Level-appropriate.** When signing up, you choose your level: Apprentice, Skilled worker – the content adjusts accordingly.

## What you don\'t get

No third-party advertising, no daily emails, no sharing of your address. Unsubscribing is possible with one click in every email; afterwards, your address will be deleted. Details in the [Datenschutzerklärung](/en/rechtliches/datenschutz/).
