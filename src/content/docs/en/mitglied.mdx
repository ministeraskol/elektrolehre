Being a member at wattwas means: You receive emails that will help you progress in your Ausbildung (apprenticeship) – and you help decide what will be explained next.

<Mitglied />

## What you get

- **New tutorials and videos first.** What appears on the site, you already have in your email.
- **Exam sprint.** Before the Zwischenprüfung (intermediate exam) and Gesellenprüfung (journeyman exam): five days, five emails – the mistakes that most commonly cost points in exams, and the rules you need to know by heart.
- **Your questions.** You can reply to every email. The most frequent questions become tutorials, videos, and quizzes.
- **Level-appropriate.** When registering, you choose Start, Azubi (apprentice), or Skilled worker – the content adapts to you.

## What you don't get

No third-party advertising, no daily emails, no sharing of your address. Unsubscribing works with one click in every email; afterwards, your address will be deleted. Details in the [Privacy Policy](./rechtliches/datenschutz/).
