**wattwas** explains electrical engineering the way you need it in your Ausbildung (apprenticeship): short, with a schema, with a source – and in the language you think best in. For Azubis (apprentices), career changers, students, hobby tinkerers and everyone who wants to know what happens behind the Steckdose (power socket).

## Where you can find wattwas

New content appears first as a short video. This site is the reference work behind it.

<Kanaele gross />

## Why it exists

Most explanation sites for electrical engineering are only in German, but many Azubis speak Turkish, Arabic, Russian or something else at home. wattwas keeps the **German Fachbegriff (technical term)** in every language – because you need it in the exam – and explains everything around it in your language. There's also **Leichte Sprache** (plain language) for everyone who is just starting to learn German.

## What wattwas is not

No DIY instructions. Only Elektrofachkräfte (qualified electrical professionals) may work on electrical installations; during the Ausbildung always under supervision. Details in the [liability disclaimer](./rechtliches/haftungsausschluss/).

## How wattwas is funded

- **No account, no tracking.** All texts are published under CC BY-SA 4.0 – you may share and reuse them, with attribution.
- **Channels:** Every subscription and every follow helps, because reach is what makes the videos possible.
- **Membership:** Anyone who [becomes a member](./mitglied/) gets new guides first and exam sprints by email – and gets a say in what gets explained next.
- **Tool guide:** Recommendations may in future contain partner links (marked with *). If you buy through them, wattwas receives a small commission – the price stays the same for you. Reviews remain independent of this.

## Get involved

Found a mistake, a term is missing, the language sounds clunky? Write a note via
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ministeraskol/elektrolehre/issues) – no programming skills needed, one sentence is enough.
Native speakers who want to proofread a machine-translated page are especially welcome.
