**wattwas** explains electrical engineering the way you need it in your Ausbildung (apprenticeship): short, with a diagram, with a source – and in the language you think best in. For Azubis (apprentices), career changers, students, hobby tinkerers and everyone who wants to know what happens behind the Steckdose (socket outlet).

## Where you can find wattwas

New content appears first as a short video. This site is the reference work behind it.

<Kanaele gross />

## Why it exists

Most electrical engineering explainer sites are only in German, but many Azubis speak Turkish, Arabic, Russian or something else at home. wattwas keeps the **German Fachbegriff (technical term)** in every language – because that's what you need in the exam – and explains everything around it in your language. There is also **Leichte Sprache (plain language)** for anyone who is just starting to learn German.

## What wattwas is not

No instructions for DIY. Only Elektrofachkräfte (qualified electricians) may work on electrical installations; during the Ausbildung always under supervision. Details in the [liability disclaimer](./rechtliches/haftungsausschluss/).

## How wattwas is funded

- **Free, no account, no tracking.** All texts are published under CC BY-SA 4.0 – you may share and reuse them, with attribution.
- **Channels:** Every subscription and every follow helps, because reach is what makes the videos possible.
- **Tool guide:** Recommendations may in future contain affiliate links (marked with *). If you buy through them, wattwas receives a small commission – the price stays the same for you. Reviews remain independent of this.
