## Two Types of Protection

Schutzorgane (protective devices) protect either the **system** (cable, devices) or the **person** – some do both.

| Danger | Protection by | Component |
|---|---|---|
| **Overload** – too many consumers, cable gets warm | Switching off at too high Strom (current) over a longer period | LS-Schalter (circuit breaker), Schmelzsicherung (fuse) |
| **Short circuit** – L touches N or PE, huge Strom | Switching off in milliseconds | LS-Schalter, Schmelzsicherung, SLS (selective main circuit breaker) |
| **Fault current / electric shock** – Strom flows via housing or person to earth | Switching off at a small difference between outgoing and returning Strom | RCD (residual current device) |
| **Overvoltage** – lightning, switching operations in the grid | Diverting the voltage peak to earth | SPD (Überspannungsschutz (surge protection) device) |

## Leitungsschutzschalter (LS-Schalter)

The **LS-Schalter** (colloquially "circuit breaker") protects the **cable** from overload and short circuits. It has two triggers:

- **Thermal trigger** (bimetal): reacts to overload – slowly, depending on how far the Strom is above the nominal value. 1.45 times the nominal Strom must trip within one hour.
- **Magnetic trigger**: reacts to short circuits – immediately, when the Strom reaches a multiple of the nominal Strom.

The labeling, for example **B16**, means:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **B** | Tripping characteristic: magnetic tripping at 3 to 5 times the nominal Strom. Standard for apartments. |
| **C** | Tripping at 5 to 10 times the nominal Strom – for motors, devices with high inrush current. |
| **D** | 10 to 20 times – transformers, welding equipment. |
| **16** | Nominal Strom 16 A |

Assignment in the apartment: **B10** for light (1.5 mm²), **B16** for Steckdose (socket) (2.5 mm² or 1.5 mm² for short cables), **B16 3-pole** for the stove (400 V, 5×2.5 mm²), **B16** or **B20** for instantaneous water heaters depending on Leistung (power) and cross-section.

The nominal Strom must fit the **cable cross-section** and the **installation method** – the cable must permanently carry the Strom that the LS lets through. This is a calculation, not a rule of thumb.

## Schmelzsicherung

The **Schmelzsicherung** (Neozed D01/D02 in the distributor, NH-fuse in the Hausanschluss (house connection) box) is the older brother of the LS-Schalter: a wire melts at overcurrent. It is cheap, robust and can switch off large short-circuit currents, but must be replaced. In the Hausanschluss box and as a Vorsicherung (back-up fuse), it is still the standard; in apartment distributors, the LS has displaced it.

## RCD – the residual current circuit breaker (FI)

The **RCD** protects the **person**. It compares the Strom flowing through the Außenleiter (phase conductor) with the Strom returning via the N. Normally, the sum is zero. If Strom flows back via another path – via the PE, via a defective device, via a person – a **difference** arises. The RCD switches off starting from the **rated residual current IΔn**.

| Specification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IΔn = 30 mA** | Personal protection. Mandatory for Steckdose up to 32 A and lighting in apartments (DIN VDE 0100-410). Tripping at the latest after 300 ms, in practice 20–40 ms. |
| **IΔn = 300 mA** | Fire protection, no personal protection. In agriculture, for entire systems. |
| **Type AC** | recognizes only sinusoidal AC fault currents – **not permitted** in Germany |
| **Type A** | additionally pulsating DC fault currents – **standard** for apartments |
| **Type F** | additionally mixed frequencies – washing machines, heat pumps with frequency converters |
| **Type B** | additionally smooth DC fault currents – Wallbox (if no DC protection in the Wallbox), PV inverters without transformer |
| **4-pole 40 A** | switches L1, L2, L3, N; the nominal Strom (40 A) is the load capacity – the RCD **does not protect against overload**, it needs a Vorsicherung |

The **test button T** generates an artificial fault current. It must be pressed monthly (stated on every device) – the only test that the customer is allowed to do themselves.

**RCBO** (FI/LS combination) is an RCD and LS in one device for a single circuit. More expensive, but in the event of a fault, only one circuit fails.

## SLS – the selective main circuit breaker

The **SLS-Schalter** is located **before the meter** in the Zählerschrank (meter cabinet) (VDE-AR-N 4100). It is the Vorsicherung of the entire customer system and has the **E-characteristic**: it is **selective** relative to the LS-Schalter behind it. In the event of a short circuit in the apartment, the B16 trips, not the SLS – otherwise the whole house would be dark. Typical **3×35 A** or **3×50 A** for a single-family house, **3×63 A** with a heat pump and Wallbox.

The SLS belongs to the Zählerschrank equipment and may, although it is located before the meter, be operated by the electrical company – it is the **disconnection point** for work on the Unterverteilung (sub-distribution board).

## SPD – Überspannungsschutz

Überspannungsschutzgeräte (SPD, Surge Protective Device) divert voltage peaks before they destroy devices. Three types, three locations:

| Type | Location | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Type 1** (lightning current arrester) | Hausanschluss / Zählerschrank | direct lightning strike (only with lightning protection system) |
| **Type 2** (surge arrester) | Zählerschrank or Unterverteilung | overvoltages from the grid, remote effects |
| **Type 3** (device protection) | Steckdose, device | residual voltage for sensitive electronics |

Since DIN VDE 0100-443 and -534 (2016), Überspannungsschutz is **mandatory in new residential buildings**. The Type-2 arrester is located in the Unterverteilung directly behind the Hauptschalter (main switch) with the shortest possible cables (under 0.5 m total) to L, N and PE.

## Interaction in the Distributor

The sequence from the feed: **SLS (before the meter) → Hauptschalter → SPD → RCD → LS → circuit.** Each component has its task; none replaces another. An RCD without LS lets the cable burn up, an LS without RCD lets 20 mA flow undisturbed through a person. How this is specifically wired is shown in the guide [Unterverteilung aufbauen](../../themen/energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/unterverteilung/).
