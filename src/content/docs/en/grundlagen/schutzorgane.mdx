## Two types of protection

Protective devices protect either the **Anlage (installation)** (**Leitung (line)**, devices) or the person – some both.

| Danger | Protection by | Component |
|---|---|---|
| **Overload** – too many consumers, Leitung gets warm | Switch off at too high Strom (current) over a longer period | LS-Schalter, Schmelzsicherung |
| **Short-circuit** – L touches N or PE, huge Strom | Switch off in milliseconds | LS-Schalter, Schmelzsicherung, SLS |
| **Fehlerstrom (fault current) / electric shock** – Strom flows via housing or person to earth | Switch off at small difference between forward- and return-flowing Strom | RCD (FI-Schutzschalter) |
| **Überspannung (overvoltage)** – lightning, switching operations in the network | Divert the Spannung (voltage) peak to earth | SPD (Überspannungsschutzgerät) |

## Leitungsschutzschalter (LS-Schalter)

The **LS-Schalter** (colloquially "Sicherungsautomat") protects the **Leitung** from overload and short-circuit. It has two triggers:

- **Thermischer Auslöser (thermal trigger)** (Bimetall): reacts to overload – slowly, depending on how much the Strom is above the Nennwert (rated value). 1.45 times Nennstrom (rated current) must trip within one hour.
- **Magnetischer Auslöser (magnetic trigger)**: reacts to short-circuit – immediately when the Strom reaches a multiple of the Nennstrom.

The marking, for example **B16**, means:

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **B** | Auslösecharakteristik (trip characteristic): magnetic tripping at 3 to 5 times Nennstrom. Standard for apartments. |
| **C** | Tripping at 5 to 10 times Nennstrom – for motors, devices with high inrush current. |
| **D** | 10 to 20 times – transformers, welding equipment. |
| **16** | Nennstrom 16 A |

Assignment in the apartment: **B10** for lighting (1.5 mm²), **B16** for Steckdosen (socket outlets) (2.5 mm² or 1.5 mm² for short Leitung), **B16 3-pole** for the cooker (400 V, 5×2.5 mm²), **B16** or **B20** for instantaneous water heaters depending on Leistung (power) and Querschnitt (cross-section).

The Nennstrom must match the Leitungsquerschnitt (conductor cross-section) and the Verlegeart (installation method) – the Leitung must carry the Strom permanently that the LS lets through. That's a calculation, not a rule of thumb.

## Schmelzsicherung

The **Schmelzsicherung** (Neozed D01/D02 in the Verteiler, NH fuse in the Hausanschlusskasten) is the older brother of the LS-Schalter: A wire melts at overcurrent. It is cheap, robust and can switch off large short-circuit currents, but it must be replaced. In the Hausanschlusskasten and as Vorsicherung (back-up fuse) it is still standard; in apartment distributors the LS has displaced it.

## RCD – the residual-current circuit breaker (FI)

The **RCD** (Residual Current Device, German **FI-Schutzschalter**) protects the person. It compares the Strom that flows via the Außenleiter (line conductor) with the one that returns via the N. Normally the sum is zero. If Strom returns via another path – via the PE, via a defective device, via a person – a **Differenz** arises. From the Bemessungsdifferenzstrom IΔn the RCD trips.

| Specification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **IΔn = 30 mA** | Personal protection. Mandatory for Steckdosen up to 32 A and lighting in apartments (DIN VDE 0100-410). Tripping at latest after 300 ms, in practice 20–40 ms. |
| **IΔn = 300 mA** | Fire protection, no personal protection. In agriculture, for whole Anlagen. |
| **Typ AC** | detects only sinusoidal AC fault currents – in Germany **not permitted** |
| **Typ A** | additionally pulsating DC fault currents – **standard** for apartments |
| **Typ F** | additionally mixed frequencies – washing machines, heat pumps with frequency converter |
| **Typ B** | additionally smooth DC fault currents – Wallbox (if no DC protection in the Wallbox), PV inverter without transformer |
| **4-pole 40 A** | switches L1, L2, L3, N; the Nennstrom (40 A) is the load capacity – the RCD **does not protect against overload**, it needs a Vorsicherung |

The **Prüftaste T** creates an artificial fault current. It is to be pressed monthly (stated on every device) – the only test the customer may do themselves.

**RCBO** (FI/LS combination) is RCD and LS in one device for a single Stromkreis (circuit). More expensive, but with a fault only one circuit fails.

## SLS – the selective main line circuit breaker

The **SLS-Schalter** sits **in front of the meter** in the Zählerschrank (VDE-AR-N 4100). It is the Vorsicherung of the whole Kundenanlage (customer installation) and has the **E-Charakteristik**: It is **selective** to the LS switches behind it. With a short-circuit in the apartment the B16 trips, not the SLS – otherwise the whole house would be dark. Typical **3×35 A** or **3×50 A** for a detached house, **3×63 A** with heat pump and Wallbox.

The SLS belongs to the Zählerschrank equipment and, although it sits in front of the meter, may be operated by the electrical contractor – it is the **Freischaltpunkt** for work on the Unterverteilung (sub-distribution board).

## SPD – Überspannungsschutz

**Überspannungsschutzgeräte** (SPD, Surge Protective Device) divert voltage peaks before they destroy devices. Three types, three locations:

| Type | Location | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Typ 1** (Blitzstromableiter) | Hausanschluss / Zählerschrank | direct lightning strike (only with lightning protection system) |
| **Typ 2** (Überspannungsableiter) | Zählerschrank or Unterverteilung | overvoltages from the network, remote effects |
| **Typ 3** (Geräteschutz) | Steckdose, device | residual voltage for sensitive electronics |

Since DIN VDE 0100-443 and -534 (2016) Überspannungsschutz is **mandatory in new residential buildings**. The Type 2 arrester sits in the Unterverteilung directly behind the Hauptschalter (main switch) with as short leads as possible (under 0.5 m total) to L, N and PE.

## Interaction in the Verteiler

The order from supply: **SLS (in front of the meter) → Hauptschalter → SPD → RCD → LS → Stromkreis.** Each component has its task; none replaces another. An RCD without LS lets the Leitung glow, an LS without RCD lets 20 mA flow undisturbed through a person. How this is wired in practice is shown in the guide [Unterverteilung aufbauen](../../themen/energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/unterverteilung/).
