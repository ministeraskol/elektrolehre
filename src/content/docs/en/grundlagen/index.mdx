Four chapters, in this order. Each chapter ends with a quiz. What you read here applies to every Fachrichtung (specialist field) – Hausinstallation (domestic installation), Automatisierung (automation), Industrie (industry) or Kfz (automotive).

## How to use the Lernpfad (learning path)

1. **Read a chapter in one go** – 5 to 8 minutes.
2. **Do the quiz** at the end. Getting something wrong is okay – the explanation is right below it.
3. **Look up terms**: Every marked Fachbegriff (technical term) takes you to the [Glossar (glossary)](../glossar/).
4. After that: the first [guide](../themen/energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/unterverteilung/) – there you'll see what you need all of this for.

Your browser remembers the chapters you've read (locally only, no account).
